Move the cursor around when typing commands.

- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>a</kbd>: Go to the beginning of the line
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>e</kbd>: Go to the end the of the line
- <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>b</kbd>: Go back one word
- <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>f</kbd>: Go forward one word
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>xx</kbd>: Move between the beginning of the line and the current position of the cursor
